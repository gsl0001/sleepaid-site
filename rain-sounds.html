Moonrain

Rain sounds for sleep

Steady rain is one of the most popular sounds to fall asleep to: it is even, it has no tune to follow, and it covers sudden noises from the street or the next room.

Moonrain is a free sleep-sound app for iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs. It works completely offline: no account, no ads, no subscription and no tracking.

Sounds in the app

  • Window Rain
  • Heavy Rain
  • Gentle Rain
  • Tent Rain
  • Spring Rain
  • Distant Thunder

You can play up to four of Moonrain's 28 sounds at once, each with its own volume, and set a sleep timer that fades everything out.

Mixes to try

  • Window Rain with a little Brown Noise underneath for a deeper, softer sound
  • Heavy Rain with Distant Thunder for a summer storm
  • Gentle Rain with Fireplace for a cosy wet night
