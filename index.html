Moonrain: Sleep Sounds

Fall asleep to rain on the window, ocean waves or a crackling fire, unwind with whispered ASMR, or focus in a quiet café or library.

Moonrain is free and works completely offline: no account, no ads, no subscription and no tracking.

What it does

  • 28 ambient sounds: rain, ocean, forest, fire, fans, trains, and brown, pink and white noise
  • Mix up to four sounds with independent volumes
  • A sleep timer that fades everything out once you're asleep
  • Relax: wind down, body scan, a breathing guide with a soft tone, and 8 whispered ASMR experiences in 3D sound
  • A meditation timer with Tibetan, crystal and deep singing bowls
  • Environments to work, study or read in: coffee shop, library, park, rainy tea house and train, plus a noise lab
  • A gentle wake alarm that fades in a nature sound
  • A dim bedside clock with a red night light, and an OK-to-wake clock for kids
  • Day Sleeper mode for people who sleep during the day
  • Save mixes, share them as a link or QR code, back them up, or export one as an audio file
  • Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, Control Center controls and Siri
  • Simple Mode with large text and one big play button
  • Works on iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every sound is built into the app, so it works in airplane mode.

Does it collect my data?

No. There is no account and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Will it keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. It plays all night under lock, and you can control it from the Lock Screen.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon.

Is the app in Chinese?

The App Store page is in English and Traditional Chinese. The app interface is in English.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or sound requests: email gsl456789@gmail.com.

  • Audio stops when I lock my phone. Make sure playback is running before locking; Moonrain uses iOS background audio and keeps playing under lock.
  • The timer didn't stop the sound. The timer fades volume to silence at the end time; check the fade-out length in the timer sheet.
  • Sounds pause when my headphones disconnect. That's the Auto pause setting — turn it off in Settings if you prefer.
