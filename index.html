Sleep Aid

A calm, offline sound mixer for sleep, relaxation, and focus. Mix up to four ambient sounds, set a sleep timer with a gentle fade-out, and let it play all night — no account, no internet, no tracking.

Common questions

  • Audio stops when I lock my phone. Make sure playback is running before locking; Sleep Aid uses iOS background audio and keeps playing under lock.
  • The timer didn't stop the sound. The timer fades volume to silence at the end time; check the fade-out length in the timer sheet.
  • Sounds pause when my headphones disconnect. That's the Auto pause setting — turn it off in Settings if you prefer.
