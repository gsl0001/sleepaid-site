Privacy policy

Effective July 10, 2026

The short version

Moonrain collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

What the app stores

Your preferences — favorites, saved mixes, volumes, timer settings — are stored only on your device, using Apple's standard on-device storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No network requests — the app works fully offline
  • No access to your microphone, location, contacts, photos, or health data
  • No data sharing with anyone, because no data is collected
