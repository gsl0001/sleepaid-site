Questions

Is there a sleep sounds app with no subscription?

Moonrain: Sleep Sounds is one. It is free on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Is anything locked behind a payment?

No. There is nothing to buy in the app. All 28 sounds, the mixer, the sleep timer, the ASMR, the meditation timer, the Environments and the wake alarm are included.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-up. You open the app and tap play.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Every sound is built into the app and the app makes no network connections, so it works offline, including in airplane mode.

What data does it collect?

None. There are no analytics and no tracking. Saved mixes, favorites, timer settings and preferences are stored only on your device.
