Moonrain

Ocean wave sounds for sleep

Waves rolling in and drawing back have a slow, regular rhythm that many people find easy to drift off to.

Moonrain is a free sleep-sound app for iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs. It works completely offline: no account, no ads, no subscription and no tracking.

Get Moonrain on the App Store

Sounds in the app

  • Ocean Waves
  • Soft Wind
  • River
  • Brown Noise

You can play up to four of Moonrain's 28 sounds at once, each with its own volume, and set a sleep timer that fades everything out.

Mixes to try

  • Ocean Waves with a low Brown Noise to smooth out the gaps between waves
  • Ocean Waves with Soft Wind for a breezy night beach
  • Ocean Waves with Gentle Rain for rain over the sea
