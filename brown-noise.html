Moonrain

Brown, pink and white noise

Noise colours differ in where their energy sits: white noise is bright and hissy, pink noise is softer, and brown noise is a deep, low rumble. Fans and air conditioners are a more familiar version of the same idea.

Moonrain is a free sleep-sound app for iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs. It works completely offline: no account, no ads, no subscription and no tracking.

You can play up to four of Moonrain's 28 sounds at once, each with its own volume, and set a sleep timer that fades everything out.

Mixes to try

  • Brown Noise with Window Rain for a low, steady bed of sound
  • Pink Noise with Gentle Rain for something softer
  • Deep Fan on its own if you're used to sleeping with a fan on
